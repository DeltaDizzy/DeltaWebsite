title: Habitable Worlds Observatory
description: The Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO or HabWorlds) is a future NASA Great Observatory. It will observe in the Near-IR, Visible, and UV bands and will image at least 25 Earth-sized exoplanets around Sunlike stars. It is in pre-formulation ahead of Mission Concept Review in 2030 and launch around 2040.
