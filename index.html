WPILib

WPILib is a C++/Java/Python robotics library used by the FIRST Robotics Competiton and soon the FIRST Tech Challenge high school robotics competitions. It enables students to focus on high level behaviors instead of managing hardware, raising the performance floor without lowering the ceiling.
